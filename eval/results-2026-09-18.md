## Verdict

**30B-A3B is the new production default** — switched in `api-service/.env`
(`OLLAMA_MODEL=qwen3-vl:30b-a3b-instruct`) on 2026-09-18.

Why it wins despite the owner's earlier bad experience with "the 30B":

- **Accuracy +4.0pp** (77.5% vs 73.5%) and it wins 5 of 8 fixtures outright.
- **Latency −33%** (126 s vs 188 s per photo). It is a MoE with only ~3.3B
  active parameters, so it decodes faster than the dense 8B on the same
  silicon — the earlier pain was the 19 GB download filling the *disk*, not
  the model starving *RAM*. With ~200 GB free disk and 19 GB weights on a
  48 GB machine, it stays resident without pushing the box into swap
  (system-wide free memory 70% after the run; API eval ran the whole time).
- Consistent with the historical number: CLAUDE.md records ~79.3% for this
  model on the 8-fixture oracle (2026-09-04 conditions).

## Reverting

## Quality tier: NVIDIA NIM nemotron-3-nano-omni-30b-a3b (2026-09-25)

The env-gated `QUALITY_VLM_*` path (`?tier=quality`) was validated end-to-end
against NVIDIA's free NIM tier: `nvidia/nemotron-3-nano-omni-30b-a3b-reasoning`
(distilled from Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B-Instruct), OpenAI-compatible, $0.

Pool availability was the blocker all week: `ResourceExhausted 16/16 workers`
on 9/18 evening and 9/19 (0/3 vision probes), finally free 9/25 (3/3) — the
full 8-fixture production-pipeline eval ran the same day
(`report-2026-09-25-00-54-55.json`).

Result: **avg 21.6% — a timeout artifact, not model quality.** 5 of 8 fixtures
hit the 600 s per-call timeout on pass 1 (the fail-loud semantics now emit a
terminal SSE error; the harness gained matching honesty the same day — an
`event: error` stream counts as a failure, not a 0-item success). The three
fixtures that completed show the model is capable: dining_room_01 **100%**
recall (25 items, 18 min end-to-end), 1776174188547 and mockup_living_01
36.4% (8 items; the latter took 29 min).

Verdict: the NIM free tier is not viable as the quality tier even when the
pool is free — per-call latency 3–29 min against a 10-min budget. To actually
serve the 30B class: bring the committed Azure A100 stack online
(`infra/azure/`, Tailscale-joined — not on the tailnet as of 9/25) or point
`QUALITY_VLM_*` at OpenRouter's hosted Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B (~$15/mo, two env
vars). The code path, tests, and mobile plumbing are already in place.

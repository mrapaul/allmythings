# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: March 20, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **All My Things**. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Inventory Data:** Images of your items and the metadata you provide (labels, categories, descriptions).
- **Vision AI Processing:** When you use our "Detect" feature, images are sent to our API service for object identification.
- **Local Data:** Most of your inventory data is stored locally on your device.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- To provide the core functionality of the app (inventory management).
- To improve our AI detection models through aggregated, non-personally identifiable data.
- To sync your data across devices (if enabled).

## 4. Data Storage and Security
- We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data.
- Your data is stored on your device and, if applicable, on our secure production servers.

## 5. Third-Party Services
We use third-party Vision AI providers (like Google Gemini and AWS Rekognition) to process images. These providers have their own privacy policies.

## 6. Your Rights
You have the right to access, delete, or modify your data at any time through the app's settings.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@allmythings.app.

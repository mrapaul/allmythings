# Product Guidelines: Visual Home Inventory Manager

## 1. Visual Identity & Aesthetic

### 1.1 Minimalist & Clean
- **Concept:** Content-first design that mimics high-end social media platforms (e.g., Instagram, Pinterest).
- **Execution:**
    - Generous use of whitespace to prevent information overload.
    - Subtle, multi-layered drop shadows to create a sense of depth ("lifted" cards).
    - Refined typography with emphasized hierarchy (large hero text, subtle labels).
    - Subtle noise textures on backgrounds for a premium, tactile feel.

### 1.2 Icon-Driven & Implicit
- **Concept:** Minimize text clutter to maintain a premium look.
- **Execution:**
    - Rely on high-quality, recognizable icons for primary actions.
    - Use gesture-based navigation (swipes, long-press) to reduce visual chrome.
    - Labels should be secondary to visual elements, often appearing only when relevant.

## 2. Voice & Messaging

### 2.1 Casual & Friendly
- **Concept:** Accessible and approachable language that makes inventory management feel light.
- **Tone:** Conversational and helpful, avoiding overly technical or clinical jargon.
- **Terminology:** Use relatable terms like "Your Things" or "Quick Snap" instead of "Asset Catalog" or "Record Entry."

## 3. Interaction & AI Experience

### 3.1 Seamless AI Integration
- **Non-Intrusive Suggestions:** AI detections should appear as subtle overlays or suggestions that don't interrupt the user's flow.
- **Real-Time Responsiveness:** The interface must feel fluid and responsive, providing immediate visual feedback during capture without blocking actions.

### 3.2 Fluid Correction Loop
- **Contextual Editing:** Corrections to AI-generated data should happen directly within the visual feed or item view.
- **Zero-Form Policy:** Avoid taking users to separate, heavy "input form" screens. Every edit should feel like an integrated part of the visual experience.

## 4. Design Standards
- **Responsiveness:** All layouts must adapt perfectly to various screen sizes while maintaining the high-end aesthetic.
- **A11Y:** Ensure accessibility (high contrast, semantic labels) is integrated without compromising the minimalist look.
